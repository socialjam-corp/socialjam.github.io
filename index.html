Unlimited, endless fun all in one app

skeep brings all your favourite single player minigames and multplayer turn-based games to your pocket!
Play what you like, and skeep what you don't!
Scroll, play or start a match with friends in seconds, no downloads per game, no waiting around.

What is skeep?

  • Play logic, reflexes or dexterity-based games curated for quick play.
  • Challenge friends directly in turn-based games to see who is the very best.
  • Compete against all your friends daily to see who holds the highest score in an endless, free-for-all game-mode.

    • PLAY

    Scroll and play through all your favourite minigames in a single app! Our propietary algorythm will curate the selection so you always play the ones you like the most at an adequate difficulty!

    CHALLENGE

    Challenge your friends to play turn-based minigames and find out who the better player!
    Your historic score is kept in the app, so you will never have doubts on who the very best is!

    COMPETE

    Create group with your friends and compete in daily challenge to see who can get the highest score in the free for all games.
    Earn points based on your position in the ranking at the end of the day. The player with the most points will be crowned the victor after the season ends.
